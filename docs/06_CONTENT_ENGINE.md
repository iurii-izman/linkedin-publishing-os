# Content Engine, Evidence and Voice

## 1. Purpose

The engine should demonstrate how the owner thinks and works. It should not generate generic thought leadership or rewrite news.

```text
concrete problem
+ verified experience or project evidence
+ mechanism or decision
+ practical conclusion
= publishable post
```

## 2. Positioning hierarchy

1. Systems analyst with commercial CRM and integration experience.
2. Bitrix24 and 1C integration specialist.
3. Process automation and data-quality practitioner.
4. Applied AI/CRM builder as a differentiator.
5. Broader engineering as supporting proof.

## 3. Evidence hierarchy

1. direct commercial experience;
2. transferable commercial experience;
3. one relevant portfolio project;
4. verified certificate;
5. public technical source;
6. clearly labeled personal observation.

## 4. Claim rules

Commercial wording may describe real Bitrix24/1C integration, field maps, synchronization rules, requirements, developer handoff and acceptance.

Portfolio wording may describe a public prototype, test-portal validation, review gates, dry-run or architecture.

Portfolio wording must not claim client production, measured outcomes, enterprise operation or commercial AI delivery.

## 5. Content pillars

- CRM and Bitrix24 — 30%
- integrations and data quality — 30%
- systems analysis and acceptance — 20%
- applied AI automation — 20%

## 6. Structure patterns

- mistake and prevention mechanism;
- decision log;
- pre-launch integration checklist;
- portfolio lesson with explicit boundary;
- comparison with decision signals.

Do not force one universal template.

## 7. Voice

- calm and specific;
- ordinary verbs;
- one main idea;
- mostly short sentences;
- clear professional English B2 or natural Russian;
- no inflated praise, manufactured controversy, generic hype or engagement bait.

## 8. Hard QA red flags

- unsupported numbers;
- `production` near portfolio claim;
- client/team/budget without evidence;
- confidential system names or payloads;
- secret-like strings;
- unrelated technology catalogue;
- generic AI-news summary;
- unsupported LinkedIn algorithm claim;
- autonomous engagement promise.

## 9. Source policy

Candidate sources:

- CV;
- public GitHub README/release;
- certification repository;
- owner notes;
- manually approved commercial abstractions.

External sources give context, not owner experience.

Private sources require explicit public-safety review and abstraction.

## 10. Similarity

Compare opening, semantic similarity, claim combination, pillar and closing against prior 90 days. High similarity is a warning before approval.

## 11. Language

English: clear B2, no idiom inflation, standard CRM/API terms.

Russian: natural professional language, no bureaucratic chains.

Do not publish bilingual duplicates in one post by default.

## 12. Hashtags and links

- 0–3 hashtags;
- one public link maximum by default;
- do not attach GitHub to every post;
- links must match the discussed evidence.
